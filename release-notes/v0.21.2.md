## Keep-alive fix for reverse proxies

v0.21.2 fixes failed requests behind nginx and Caddy. No configuration changes; if your proxy keeps upstream connections longer than 120 s, lower that.

## Changes

- **Idle keep-alive connections are kept for 120 s instead of 30 s.** A reverse proxy that holds upstream connections open longer than the server does (nginx and Caddy with the `deploy/` configs keep them for 60 s) reused connections the server had already closed, and those requests failed with "connection reset by peer" or a 502, in bursts after quiet periods. The `deploy/nginx/vaultbase.conf` comment notes the constraint.

## Upgrade notes

- The proxy's upstream keepalive timeout must stay below 120 s. The shipped nginx and Caddy configs use 60 s and need no change.
